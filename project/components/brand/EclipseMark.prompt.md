The standalone eclipse (two overlapping circles). Use as a quiet signature — favicon, app/social avatar, packaging seal, end-of-article mark — never as decoration or a repeating pattern.

```jsx
<EclipseMark size={24} />
<EclipseMark size={64} title="Luma" />
```

- Colours are fixed (orange front, peach behind) on every background.
- Never add rays, outlines, gradients or a third circle. Minimum 12px.
